Scene countdown ring

Mockup · one ring, in the master strip of the mixer row · Loom

What it does

One circle. It shows how much of the current scene is left before the switch instant — the moment Loom already computes in sceneSwitchBoundary(). Idle, it just breathes with the scene's loop. Queue another scene and it turns amber and counts that same loop down to zero, naming where you're going.

Three readings of the same number

A · Swept sector
Ableton's pie. Filled wedge grows over the loop; empties over the countdown.
B · Depleting arc
Thin ring drains anticlockwise. Quieter, reads as "remaining", not "elapsed".
C · Bar segments
One tick per bar of the governing loop. Countable at a glance, no number needed.
